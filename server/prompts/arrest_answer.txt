No, not automatically. SAPA 129 records your report and connects you with the local protection service (UPTD PPA), which can offer counselling, a health check, legal help and a safe place to stay.

A police report is the usual way a criminal case starts. But if a situation looks high-risk, staff may work with police, and for some offences police can act once they know about it.

You can ask the counsellor what they would do in your situation.
